8. Box-Sizing Comparison Page

Both boxes declare 260px width and equal padding. content-box adds padding and borders outside that width; border-box includes them.

content-box

The declared width applies to content only. Total width is 260px plus left/right padding and borders.

border-box

The declared width includes content, padding, and borders, so total width remains 260px.
