7. Typography and Text Styling

This short article demonstrates font families, weights, sizes, spacing, alignment, and styled inline elements.

The Art of Clear Communication

Good typography makes information easier to understand. Font family, size, and weight help readers recognize headings and body text quickly.

Readable paragraphs benefit from comfortable line spacing and carefully chosen letter spacing. Use strong emphasis for important ideas and emphasis to add nuance without overwhelming the reader.

A serif typeface can create an editorial feeling, while a sans-serif typeface often feels clean and modern.
